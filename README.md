# Lunar Solar Power Network (LSPN)

## Project Overview

Lunar Solar Power Network (LSPN) is a conceptual system designed to explore how solar energy could be collected, stored, monitored, and distributed on the Moon.

The concept uses modular solar power units, robotic deployment and maintenance, energy storage, power distribution, and monitoring systems.

## Main Objectives

- Generate renewable energy on the lunar surface.
- Use robotic systems to deploy and maintain solar modules.
- Store energy for periods when sunlight is unavailable.
- Monitor the system and detect possible faults.
- Protect equipment from the lunar environment, including dust and extreme temperatures.

## Proposed Development Stages

1. Computer modeling and simulation.
2. Earth-based robotic and energy-system testing.
3. Vacuum and environmental testing.
4. Small-scale demonstration.

## Future Potential

LSPN could be investigated as a possible supporting energy infrastructure for future lunar exploration and scientific facilities.

# LSPN System Design

## 1. Concept

The Lunar Solar Power Network (LSPN) is a conceptual lunar energy infrastructure designed to collect solar energy, store it, and distribute electrical power to future lunar equipment and facilities.

## 2. Main Components

### Solar Power Units
Modular photovoltaic panels collect sunlight on the lunar surface.

### Energy Storage
Battery or other energy-storage systems store electricity for periods when solar generation is reduced or unavailable.

### Robotic Maintenance
Robotic systems could inspect, clean, repair, and reposition equipment where practical.

### Power Distribution
A modular electrical network distributes generated power to scientific instruments, communication systems, and other future lunar infrastructure.

### Monitoring System
Sensors continuously monitor temperature, voltage, current, battery status, and equipment condition.

## 3. Basic Energy Model

A simplified estimate can be represented as:

Power = Solar Irradiance × Panel Area × Panel Efficiency

For example, a future simulation could compare different panel areas and efficiencies to estimate energy production.

## 4. Lunar Environment

The design must consider:

- Extreme temperature changes
- Lunar dust
- Radiation
- Reduced gravity
- Long periods without sunlight in some locations
- Reliability and autonomous operation

## 5. Development Path

1. Computer simulation
2. Small-scale prototype
3. Robotic testing
4. Environmental testing
5. Future lunar demonstration

## 6. Project Status

This is an early-stage conceptual student project. The calculations and engineering specifications require further research, simulation, and testing.
